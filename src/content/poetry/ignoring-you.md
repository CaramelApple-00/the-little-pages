You make it impossible to ignore you\
Or maybe it's that the thought you fell like summer's dew\
right where my mind needed you and so now I'm Jack staring at the beanstalk that overnight grew

Because when you spend hours upon hours working on a dream\
that's worth the entire class of chemistry\
you start to notice that the proximity starts to make me- us the alchemists that brewed the tiny seed from which sprung a bean

And the fast pace to grab coffee and head to our seats\
like we're not ten minutes past when we needed to be\
our steps perfectly synced out of sync so we don't quite walk over the brim of tension we refuse to drink

Because just because we head to and fro at the same time doesn't mean we're going and coming together\
It just means it's a little harder to ignore and stay silent each time

And so the rush and care that consumes you when I hover over broken glass like the fingers that may get cut are those of your muse\
The concern to do as much as you can so the pain from my side no longer makes me bend over and cry\
Fills me with the need to pry

Because if taking my seat wasn't enough, and the care wasn't enough, then your smile was more than enough to force me to stop my feeble attempt to ignore,\
but you already know that, because if you make yourself, and I make you so hard to ignore, then I must be the same for you
