---
title: "An Open Door"
chapter: "in the feels"
date: 2026-10-26
note: "I think this is my poem of the year; you won't get a better one out of me. But this is for when you're rediscovering the dance steps you thought you forgot, and learning to live life like you did when you were six, and the world was colourful."
---

Six year old me loved to dance\
She loved to sing, to swing on the stair rails to a melody\
The melody that painted love as an open door\
And she sang along, believing that her Prince Charming would swing through, hers ever more

16 year old me forgot the steps to her favourite dance\
She sang a little less, the rails stopped their creaking\
And instead of waiting at that painted doorframe\
She begged the passing knaves to enter, hoping their shrewd hearts she could tame

18 year old me followed someone else's dance steps\
She couldn't sing anymore, she couldn't let out her fears\
One brute ripping her voice away meant her Prince Charming didn't exist\
Her carefully decorated door seemingly smashed to bits

But 19 year old me found 6 year old me's dance steps\
She sings a little more, and the orange cat meows in harmony with her\
She opened her door once more, but she doesn't beg anymore\
-no she's grown\
She might peek out once in a while,\
her cheeky smile and tiny fangs she's come to adore in full view\
and she waits for her Prince Charming to come through\
She thinks he's found her, waiting at the door with flowers,\
so she runs through her door to the meadows she dreams of\
because at last, love's an open door
