I've seen the way you look at me,\
and I can't help but smile at the glint in your eyes\
it takes me back to the childhood game, when time seemed to fly\
I know you're good at chasing me,\
but catch me if you can

See the chase is fun and all,\
and you're no stranger to the race,\
but if you've kept up the pace so far,\
then why draw out the chase?\
I've shown my cards, even my last ace,\
this time I'll make it fair\
so catch me if you care

And it's fun to leap over the bonfire\
to feel the flames lick your feet as they climb higher,\
the rush that fills your lungs,\
even after your feet kiss the sand,\
And if it's the other side I choose to stand by—\
to run away\
Maybe reach over for my hand, leap\
and catch me if you dare\
but maybe that's the thing about falling

if we're playing trust falls,\
I know you've always caught me,\
even when I've fought to be free\
of love, of trust, anything that makes us "would bes"\
but if you're wondering whether I'll let you catch me\
I've seen the love you long for yet lack,\
I'll let you catch me, and I won't look back
