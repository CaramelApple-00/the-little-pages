title: "My name"
chapter: "being me"

There was a time my name carried pride\
mama called me, and suddenly two syllables turned into\
"God has answered my prayers"\
And for a while, my name spoken was a prayer breathed— in holy words enwreathed

But yevu saw my name as hard,\
and suddenly I learned, to them,\
Zbigniew was easier to speak\
easier than acknowledging the rich clay from which mine was moulded,\
or nodding to the name with which was written golden

So a time came when I took on many names but my own,\
any name to rise above without yevu asking which hut I called my home\
and the name once prayer, turned into Cain's curse\
casting a shadow where I walked,\
and shame standing tall, gawked

even still time came and went again,\
but this time I wondered,\
Why is my name such a stain?\
why do they rise with less kra to their name,\
yet mine is the one that can't reign?!\
disturbed, I couldn't wait for the sun to pass on me again\
the ache and urge I could not curb

So the prayer that became my name, I dusted off\
I picked it back, for even still with my name I will rise\
And I will strive to rise not just survive with the name that echoes the joy of my land, my home, my mind and peace\
Because my name is not only my crown, it is my cry\
And with the ones that went before on my shoulders, I go with my name\
And even still, I rise
