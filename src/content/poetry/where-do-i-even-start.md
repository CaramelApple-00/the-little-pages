---
title: "Where do I even start?"
chapter: "in the feels"
note: "I think the title speaks for itself. But how do I say we're meant for each other, in every which way, without sounding out of my mind?"
---

How do I explain to you that we're puzzle pieces without sounding crazy?\
That every path we've taken is more just than a college maze?\
There's too many paths and doors shut that brought me to the one open door that doesn't look hazy\
And after trying so hard to ignore it there's just one thing I can't ignore\
There's a lot actually

Because since God's got jokes he wrapped you in the one thing I vowed to run from\
Into a miracle that's left me nothing but stunned\
Except what's inside is everything that complements the person He moulded me into

See there goes a story that long ago we used to be connected\
And I don't know if we shared a heart,\
but when the jealous gods cleaved us in half I'm pretty sure it ripped mine regardless\
That I was doomed to spend my life trying to find the one with whom my heart felt calmness\
But then I found you

But two souls cleaved after creation might not look the way we dreamed\
because when you wait on a promise for so long you start to forget\
that the prayer He heard first was the little girl with dreams\
And that he moulded you into her puzzle piece

Yet I still think that we were cleaved with one heart between us\
Because the one's we've got fill the spaces of everyone else's hoping\
That maybe we'll find each other to fit each other without need to meld\
We dream the same, so much so that we're in the same fields\
And we run through meadows hoping our hearts will yield\
to the call that we feel unqualified to complete\
because we know our heart is anything but neat

But maybe you know that already\
and maybe that's why you took the first steps\
because sometimes you just know that you know the soul the gods stole,\
but didn't fling far enough to keep you out of touch, out of reach, out of sight\
and maybe yet you'll be mine to keep

So maybe you won't think I'm crazy, because if our hearts were once one\
You'd feel the fight before giving into to the beating drum\
And maybe they'll finally be at peace that we've won\
So maybe you won't think I'm crazy, because we carry each other somewhere so deep in our souls\
that even we don't know\
But even jagged and ripped in half, the heart still knows
