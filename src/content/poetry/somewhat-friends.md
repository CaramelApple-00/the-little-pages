When do I even say something\
Because I know that there's something there\
I just don't know what it is yet. Do you find me as just a young black girl you want to protect?\
Or are you just attracted to the fact that I'm pretty and nice to you?\
Or do you like me? Like really like me. I know you like me, I just don't know if you like love me.

Because somewhat friends don't get jealous when another's name comes up.\
And somewhat friends don't protect and save seats in class-well maybe they do.\
But somewhat friends don't argue to send me home in an uber, or get mad when I clean broken glass\
with nothing but nitrile gloves. I don't think somewhat friends do that.

But I don't want to ask you, because then I don't know if we'll be somewhat friends anymore, although\
I desperately want to know.

Because I want us to be more than somewhat friends who have somewhat feelings who what if their\
way into dancing on a tightrope pretending not to see the butterflies dancing below in our hearts.\
Somewhat friends aren't like us, and we're not like them, because we'll always somewhat know.
