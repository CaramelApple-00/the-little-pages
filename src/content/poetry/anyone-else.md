I wanted anyone else but you,\
Something than you to fill the hole deep down\
But lately the hole feels a little more you\
How am I supposed to think about anyone else,\
anything else, when the thought of your smile makes my heart melt?\
When holding your hand screaming forever when the rain pelts,\
or singing in the kitchen making dinner makes me think of no one else.\
But I need to think of anyone else\
ANYTHING ELSE\
You fill me a certain uncertainty-I know I'm not going to feel quite like this with another\
So now I need to think of someone else- what happens when we let the tension boil over yet do nothing about it?\
Keep on like we always do?\
I'm done pretending that I want anything else- anyone else\
No one makes my heart jump a beat of joy, or fills me with a quiet calm piece, that yet bubbles with promise, of a life filled with joy, splendor, and the patter of 4 little pairs of feet while we slow dance in the kitchen\
How am I supposed to think of anyone else?\
How do I get over my tower of what ifs to jump to your arms, because when my what ifs start to what if I wonder if we'll ever make it to each other\
But I don't need anyone else, and maybe it's time to let my what ifs fly free, and take that step
