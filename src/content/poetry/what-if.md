What if?\
I can't be bothered with my what-ifs\
Because what if I start to what if?\
And the chemistry starts to get a little more bearable\
and you stealing my seat isn't another tick?\
And what if I sat beside you, defiant that you stole my seat?\
Would that of changed our reaction rate?\
Or would that've turned us into a ruined moment of heat?\
And if I let you send me home,\
Would I have the answers I need now?\
Would we be hand in hand with me calling you my own?\
Would you be giving me the hand I yearn to hold?\
And what if I hadn't let you join our group?\
Or found someone before you put yourself out to ask?\
Would we be in this mess?\
But it's not really a mess if it hasn't even been put to the test and it could all be in my head I mean just what-if I'm just THE mess?\
And what if I stopped you in the halls, or during our morning coffee runs?\
Would it have turned into me seeing you run,\
cheering for you as I hear that pistol gun?\
And what if I didn't let the fragments get to me?\
What if I had just asked you what I am to you, what you are to me?\
What if I'm just a friend, or a little sister, or something far more than I could imagine I mean what if I'm THAT one?\
and what if my what-ifs are just the blocks building up to a fall\
that I'm swinging and skipping on the sides of that wall\
that I'll fall from my tower of what ifs to never be put back together again?\
What happens then?\
what if my what-ifs are my biggest friend and foe?\
That they keep me from running to you but don't stop me from walking either\
Because for the first time my what-ifs have ground,\
that for once a life with you seems sound,\
and I can feel the only thing stopping me is the what-if to the first what-if, and the next?\
But..\
What if the what-ifs is the last thing I see before I fall?
