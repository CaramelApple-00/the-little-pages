The heart is supposed to look like a pear

At least upside down tucked away, embraced by the arms I call a lair

It's supposed to be cozy, it's supposed to be firm

Mine doesn't

Mine is .... a basket

A basket?

A basket is meant to hold, to secure the things that were once brimming in your arms

Mine carries the fragments of my upside down pear

But my heart feels like it's only just found out that it's not whole

But I'm okay being not okay

Because at least I'm not holding the fragments in my arms

That they fall out too far to catch

At least the basket was made while His eye was on the sparrow

So I know the pieces are broken

My heart's a little slower than my mind

and I'm okay not being okay

I'm just not okay holding a basket of a thousand unshed tears

but it's okay

I'll be okay

I'm always okay

So maybe I'm not okay right now

But at least I can start looking at what fragments to put back into my upside down pear
