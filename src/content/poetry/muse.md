It's funny to see you walk around without a clue that you're my muse\
that even the way you snag the sour patches lights the fuse,\
because with you there's no need to find words to prove,\
you just are, it just is, and we just are

And when you're around there's no thing as a writer's block,\
maybe there is, but when I'm with you there's no room\
for doubt to flock\
the words, they overwhelm, flooding and washing over me and the words don't quite\
come out as fast as they appear and somehow they just are

And I didn't mean for you to mean this much,\
I actually meant to go the other way,\
but somehow some way you managed to turn my gaze\
and now every piece seems to scream of your touch\
i don't know how but they just do, and we just are

So I didn't mean for you to be my muse, but I'm glad you are\
Because words aren't really that far from reach,\
and neither are you,\
beautifully synced out of sync, it's a little chaotic but then again, that's how it is with us,\
we just are
