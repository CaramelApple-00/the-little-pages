I've spent so much time falling from my tower of what ifs,\
that I didn't really bother to ask,\
what if I forget how to write about anything else?\
the grass, the sun, sorrow and joy, or the feeling you get at the edge of a cliff?

And I'm too busy focused with who I'm falling with,\
that I didn't really bother to wonder,\
what—how do I write about anything else?\
because in truth, sometimes my words are gold, and you its smith

And I've spent too many hours looking at the sky as we fall\
admiring the canvas; the blues, the purples and oranges,\
that all I've drawn has been our golden hour—no changes\
and I don't quite know how to pen anything else

But maybe I should enjoy the fall,\
enjoy the wind in my hair, and your hand in mine,\
the sweet pine made brighter with petrichor,\
and the golden hour falling on us like ichor\
I guess what I'm trying to say is, I'm enjoying the fall, and I don't want anything else
