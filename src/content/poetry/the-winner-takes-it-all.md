How do you hate the man who stole it all?\
Because people tell me "Screw him"\
Or he's the one that blew it\
And yet still there's a little fairy jumping in the space that once held my heart\
But you stole that too\
And so every now and then I still feel your touch every time\
you open the box to admire what you've done

And how do you hate the cruel master who seems to control your very fate\
Because like a bird let out to breathe you let me roam to fill my life with cheer\
But then take it all back when you walk into the room\
I guess you took my breath too

And I'd like to say that you didn't get my soul\
But Lord knows you've been greedy to the core\
And so you took that to hoard since you know the light it hosts\
Is worth more than it's weight in gold\
So you had to steal that as you left too\
what else haven't you taken?\
What else have you refused to give up?

At some point we both need to break apart\
But until then I can't hate you\
Because you have all the parts that helps me love, heal, or even feel sane\
I can't hate you because hating you means hating the parts of me you stole\
And since you took it all,\
I can't help but wonder if we'll ever be free from our holds
