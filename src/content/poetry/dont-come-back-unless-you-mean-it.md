title: "Don't come back unless you mean it."
chapter: "finished business"
date: 2026-04-29
note: "If you're coming back, I want it all. And even if you don't, please bring my heart back. Untouched."

Don't come back unless you mean to stay in my life\
Because I can't take another day of wondering\
Is this the life I'm going to wake up to?\
That I'm second best and get dealt with the crumbs no one's come to?

When I was around you I hated the skin I was in\
the little girl inside me crying asking what was wrong with her?\
Was it her wit, her smile, or her midnight tint?\
Was it the fact that she poured so openly that you could go to and fro\
Then come back and yet still she has more to go? More to give? More to love?

See I gave so much I didn't see myself anymore,\
The little girl couldn't recognize the face staring back at her\
And so the only thing she could do was stare back at the stranger she has to accept as her\
And to keep herself fed she ate the crumbs you left for her\
Just so she could stay in the glasslike appearance you wanted

I gave you my soul, I gave you my heart, I gave you my breath\
And you took it and hid them as if the stench that followed your deceit wouldn't catch up\
As if the push and pull wouldn't come to the horsemen finding too many pieces to put back together

But the funny thing is I already forgave you before the first blow hit\
And I forgive you for the never stolen kiss\
Because like a book a kid forgets you never gave my heart back\
So if I hate you, I'm just turning what's with you to black

So if you're coming back, however which way\
Please bring my heart back\
This little girl can't face the world without her sass, her charm, or her spunk

And if you're coming back, however which way\
Come back with a loaf, not crumbs\
I'm tired of having what's left of my soul turned to dust

And if you're coming back, whatever which way\
Please mean it, because you still carry the heart this little one needs back
