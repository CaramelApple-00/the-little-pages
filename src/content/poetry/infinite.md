Like the song goes, I think I'm jealous of the rain,\
For in the city of rain your skin is hers to kiss\
She doesn't even know how lucky she is;\
when her mists kiss your lashes,\
she has you, without thought\
infinitely

I'm also jealous of the stars\
that get to look down on you,\
the ones that light up your eyes with wonder,\
and as you trace their lines they gaze back on you,\
bright blue, also in awe,\
infinite

I could be jealous of the sun,\
that warms your skin golden\
but the smile he thinks stolen\
was mine before his shine begun\
So I don't think I'm much jealous of Mr. Sun\
even if it rises still\
infinitely

But see the world will never know you as infinitely as I do,\
So the jealousy seems to fade; allayed\
and the world in all its beauty will never behold me as you do,\
Or love me like you do- infinitely\
The truth is, you're infinite, and I want you infinitely
