Hello seems like the right way to begin with. I'm Sesi. I'm a 2nd year biology student, trying to get into med school, which means I wake up for my 8AMs questioning my life choices. And obviously, I write. I've written for as long as I can remember (and vlogged, but that's besides the point), and writing gets everything else that I'd rather not say out. If you're asking why I'm not an English, Humanities or Arts major since I write so much, I'm wondering that too. I decided to put my writing, thoughts and videos and all my shenanigans after a friend said a lot of what I write and the thoughts and words her head and heart can't seem to put into words.

Little Pages is the yellow pages, but for your heart — sometimes your brain too. To crash out, to fall in love with words again. Or to just vibe...lowkey just vibe.

And I might change this intro, I might not.

Anyway, hi. Glad you're here. I hope you stay a while with me. ☺️
